Vegetarian Lettuce Wraps

.cook

🥘 Ingredients

  • baby bella cremini mushrooms
    8 oz
  • canola oil
    2 tsp
  • carrots
  • extra-firm tofu
    1 package
  • garlic
    2 cloves
  • ginger
    2 tsp
  • green onions
    4
  • hoisin sauce
    3 tbsp
  • red pepper flakes
    1/4 tsp
  • rice vinegar
    2 tbsp
  • romaine lettuce leaves
    8 large
  • sesame oil
    1 tsp
  • soy sauce
    3 tbsp
  • water chestnuts
    1 can

🍳 Cookware

  • small bowl
  • paper towels
  • large nonstick skillet
  1. 1
    In a small bowl , stir together the hoisin sauce , soy sauce , rice vinegar , and sesame oil . Set aside.
    hoisin sauce: 3 tbsp, soy sauce: 3 tbsp, rice vinegar: 2 tbsp, sesame oil: 1 tsp
  2. 2
    Press the extra-firm tofu (12- to 14-oz, do not use silken) between paper towels to squeeze out as much liquid as possible. Refresh the paper towels and press again.
    extra-firm tofu: 1 package
  3. 3
    Heat the canola oil (or grapeseed oil) in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
    canola oil: 2 tsp
  4. 4
    Once the oil is hot, crumble in the tofu, breaking it into very small pieces as it cooks.
  5. 5
    Continue cooking for ⏱️ 5 minutes , then add the finely chopped baby bella cremini mushrooms .
    baby bella cremini mushrooms: 8 oz
  6. 6
    Continue cooking until any remaining tofu liquid cooks off and the tofu starts to turn golden, about ⏱️ 3 minutes more.
  7. 7
    Stir in the drained and finely chopped water chestnuts (8 oz), minced garlic , freshly grated ginger , red pepper flakes (omit if sensitive to spice), and half of the thinly sliced green onions . Cook until fragrant, about ⏱️ 30 seconds more.
    water chestnuts: 1 can, garlic: 2 cloves, ginger: 2 tsp, red pepper flakes: 1/4 tsp, green onions: 4
  8. 8
    Pour the sauce over the top of the tofu mixture and stir to coat. Cook just until you hear bubbling and the sauce is warmed through, ⏱️ 30-60 seconds .
  9. 9
    Spoon the tofu mixture into individual romaine lettuce leaves (from a romaine heart, or butter lettuce leaves). Top with the remaining green onions, grated carrots (optional), and additional red pepper flakes (optional) as desired. Enjoy immediately.
    romaine lettuce leaves: 8 large, carrots, red pepper flakes
