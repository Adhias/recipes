Black Beans

  1. 1
    Heat olive oil in a pan .
    olive oil: 2 tbsp
  2. 2
    Add chopped onion and saute for ⏱️ 3-4 minutes .
    onion: 3 tbsp
  3. 3
    Rinse and drain black beans .
    black beans: 1 can
  4. 4
    When onions turn golden brown, add the black beans.
  5. 5
    Add taco seasoning and mix well.
    taco seasoning: 1 tsp
  6. 6
    Saute for ⏱️ 3-4 minutes more.

Avocado Dip (Guacamole)

  1. 1
    Mash avocado in a bowl using a fork .
    avocado: 1
  2. 2
    Add chopped onion , diced tomato , chopped cilantro , salt , lemon juice , and chilli powder . Mix well.
    onion: 3 slices, tomato: 1, cilantro: 1, salt: 1/4 tsp, lemon juice: 1 tsp, chilli powder: 1/4 tsp

Vegetables

  1. 1
    Thinly slice all vegetables: red pepper , green pepper , yellow pepper , zucchini , jalapeno , carrot , and onion .
    red pepper: 1, green pepper: 1, yellow pepper: 1, zucchini: 1, jalapeno: 2, carrot: 1, onion: 1
  2. 2
    Heat up olive oil in a large pan .
    olive oil
  3. 3
    Add onion slices and saute for ⏱️ 2-3 minutes .
  4. 4
    Add sliced carrots, saute for ⏱️ 5-7 minutes .
  5. 5
    Add all peppers, zucchini, and sliced mushroom .
    mushroom: 1 cup
  6. 6
    Saute everything on medium heat for ⏱️ 10 minutes or until everything looks cooked.
  7. 7
    Garnish with salt and crushed black pepper .
    salt, crushed black pepper

Fajitas

  1. 1
    Spread a bit of olive oil in a pan and slightly grill wheat tortilla .
    olive oil: 4 tbsp, wheat tortilla
  2. 2
    On each tortilla layer the following items: prepared black beans, prepared vegetables, shredded lettuce , prepared guacamole, sour cream , and salsa . Top with mexican cheese .
    lettuce: 1 cup, sour cream, salsa, mexican cheese
